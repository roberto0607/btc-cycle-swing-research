# Phase 7 Robustness Testing -- Cycle Only

Scoped to Cycle Only (RESEARCH_SPEC.md section 12.5 -- the swing layer was dropped after Milestone 9). Three checks: does performance hold across a region of the core-allocation parameters (not just the exact chosen values), does it survive a worse execution assumption, and does it hold up cycle-by-cycle rather than being carried by one spectacular period.
